Artists' Wages

The asterisks (*) on the table denote companies that pay artists (technically, their rights holders) more than $0.005 per stream. That may not sound like a lot, but the payout process for artists is complex. For reference, the best on the market, Qobuz, is currently paying $0.01873 per stream.

When deciding where to release their music, small artists need to weigh a platform's payment-per-stream against its popularity -- is it worth it to be paid more by a lesser-known streaming service, or should they take the risk and get paid less by an industry giant that everyone has heard of?

This article on Prolific Daily covers the nuances of the situation in great detail. Half way through the article is a comparison table for some of the biggest streaming services.

Switching Platforms

If you try a streaming service and it doesn't work out for you, don't worry! There are several websites that can migrate your playlists and favorites from one app to the next.

Soundiiz will transfer your data, playlist by playlist for up to 200 tracks/playlist, free of charge.
