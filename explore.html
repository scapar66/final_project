Explore

Welcome to our explore page! The tools below will allow you to search for music you already know and love, learn about new and diverse music by connecting with radio stations worldwide, and get inspired to think differently about how you organize your music with a fun random genre generator.

(1) Artist, Release, and Recording Lookup

Search for music with this service provided by the open source music encyclopedia MusicBrainz. Select artist, release, or recording in the dropdown menu and type the name of the artist, song, or album you would like to search to see the top 5 most relevant results.

(2) The Genrenator

The Binary Jazz genrenator produces unique, random genres that foster imagination and inspire creation. According to its creators, the genrenator has created over 5.5 million genres since 2018.

(3) Explore Radio Stations

Explore radio stations across the world with this service from RadioBrowser. Enter a country into the search bar to see its top 5 radio stations!
